Privacy Policy

PriAurity · Effective 25 November 2026

PriAurity does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

What the app stores

To do its job, PriAurity keeps a small amount of information on your Mac, inside the app's own sandboxed storage:

  • The names and identifiers of audio devices you've connected, and the order you've ranked them in.
  • The last volume and mute setting for each device, so they can be restored.
  • Your settings, such as whether automatic switching and open at login are on, which devices you've turned on Virtualise Volume for, each device's equaliser settings, and your sound themes.

This information never leaves your Mac. Deleting the app removes it.

Network access

PriAurity does not connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code.

Microphone

PriAurity chooses which microphone macOS uses by default, but it never opens a microphone or records or listens to what it picks up.

Virtualise Volume, equaliser, sound themes and system audio

Virtualise Volume, the equaliser and sound themes are optional features, off unless you turn them on. Virtualise Volume gives working volume control to outputs that have none or ignore their own, such as a dock's optical port. The equaliser and sound themes change how your audio sounds.

To do this, PriAurity takes the sound your apps play, adjusts its level or tone, and plays it to your device. macOS calls this recording system audio and asks your permission first. The sound only passes through PriAurity's memory on your Mac as it plays. It is never recorded, saved, analysed or sent anywhere.

You can turn these features off at any time: Virtualise Volume and the equaliser from the device's right-click menu, and sound themes by choosing Off. You can also remove the permission in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.

Children

PriAurity collects no information from anyone, including children.

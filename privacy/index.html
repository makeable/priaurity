Privacy Policy

PriAurity · Effective 25 November 2026

PriAurity does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

What the app stores

To do its job, PriAurity keeps a small amount of information on your Mac, inside the app's own sandboxed storage:

  • The names and identifiers of audio devices you've connected, and the order you've ranked them in.
  • The last volume and mute setting for each device, so they can be restored.
  • Your settings, such as whether automatic switching and launch at login are on, and which devices you've turned on Virtualise Volume for.

This information never leaves your Mac. Deleting the app removes it.

Network access

PriAurity does not connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code.

Microphone

PriAurity chooses which microphone macOS uses by default, but it never opens a microphone or records or listens to what it picks up.

Virtualise Volume and system audio

Virtualise Volume is an optional feature, off unless you turn it on for a device. It gives working volume control to outputs that ignore their own, such as a dock's optical port.

To do this, PriAurity takes the sound your apps play to that device, lowers it to the volume you've set, and plays it to the device. macOS calls this recording system audio and asks your permission first. The sound only passes through PriAurity's memory on your Mac as it plays. It is never recorded, saved, analysed or sent anywhere.

You can turn Virtualise Volume off at any time from the device's right-click menu, and remove the permission in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.

Children

PriAurity collects no information from anyone, including children.

Getting started

Click the speaker icon in the menu bar. Drag devices in the Output and Input lists into the order you prefer. PriAurity switches to the highest-ranked device that's connected, and switches again whenever devices connect or disconnect.

Common questions

Why did PriAurity switch away from the device I picked?

When you choose a device in System Settings or Control Center, PriAurity moves it to the top of your connected devices, so it stays selected. If a device ranked higher than it is connected later, that device takes over. Drag the device higher in the list to change this.

A device I've unplugged is still in the list

Unplugged devices stay in the list, greyed out, so they keep their place for next time. To remove one, right-click it and choose Forget Device.

My dock appears twice

Some docks report a different identity when plugged into a different port. PriAurity recognises most of these automatically. If you still see a duplicate, forget the unplugged copy.

The volume changed when I reconnected a device

With "Restore each device's volume" turned on, PriAurity puts back the last volume you set for that device. Adjust it once and that level is remembered.

How do I stop automatic switching for a while?

Use the switch at the top of the PriAurity menu. The menu bar icon loses its sound waves while switching is paused.

How do I start PriAurity when I log in?

Turn on "Launch at login" at the bottom of the PriAurity menu.

Some devices have no volume control

Many monitors connected over HDMI or DisplayPort don't let macOS change their volume. PriAurity can still select them, but can't remember or restore their volume.
